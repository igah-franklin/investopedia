4. PROGRAM STRUCTURE

4.1 Program Components

The InvestoVilla Pipeline Development Program for Entrepreneurs ("IPDPE") is a staged investment-readiness and fundraising support program
consisting of two primary phases:

(a) the Venture Readiness Clinic; and

(b) the Fundraising Accelerator.

=== Page 60 ===
The Program may also substitute or include additional activities, events, workshops, mentoring sessions, investor engagement opportunities,
ecosystem activities, follow-up support, or supplementary interventions as determined by InvestoVilla from time to time.



4.2 Venture Readiness Clinic

The Venture Readiness Clinic is intended to help founders strengthen the underlying fundamentals of their ventures before engaging in active
fundraising.

The Clinic may include, without limitation:

(a) Business Model Design activities;

(b) Product-Market Fit assessments;

(c) Venture Backability Assessments;

(d) Venture Design Workshops;

(e) Executive Peer Board sessions;

(f) Group facilitation activities;

(g) one-on-one consultations;

(h) review of fundraising materials;

(i) funding strategy development; and

(j) such additional activities as InvestoVilla may reasonably determine.

=== Page 61 ===
The Venture Readiness Clinic is designed on the principle that founders cannot effectively communicate an investment opportunity without first
developing a venture capable of withstanding investor scrutiny.



4.3 Venture Readiness Clinic Deliverables

Subject to satisfactory participation and compliance with Program requirements, founders participating in the Venture Readiness Clinic may receive
some or all of the following:

(a) an InvestoVilla Venture Backability Assessment™ (IVBA™);

(b) an InvestoVilla Venture Backability Report™ (IVBR™);

(c) Venture Readiness Classification outcomes;

(d) approved Lean Canvas outputs;

(e) Pitch Deck review and feedback;

(f) Funding Strategy Memoranda;

(g) Venture Design recommendations;

(h) submission opportunities to selected funding organisations actively deploying capital, where applicable; and

(i) such additional outputs as InvestoVilla may determine appropriate.

The precise nature and extent of deliverables may vary based on founder participation, Startup maturity, Program track, and operational
considerations.

=== Page 62 ===
4.4 Fundraising Accelerator

The Fundraising Accelerator is designed to provide practical fundraising execution support through structured action-learning methodologies.

The Accelerator may include:

(a) Startup Funding Fundamentals;

(b) Venture Finance Landscape education;

(c) Capital Structure Planning;

(d) Fundraising Strategy Design;

(e) Investor Psychology and Targeting;

(f) Due Diligence Preparation;

(g) Negotiation Readiness activities;

(h) Fundraising Process Management;

(i) Capstone Projects and Fundraising Hackathons;

(j) Mentorship and Coaching sessions;

(k) Peer Accountability Structures;

(l) Investor Pipeline Mapping activities; and

=== Page 63 ===
(m) such other activities as InvestoVilla may determine.

The Accelerator is intended to support founders in launching, refining, or advancing actual fundraising efforts.



4.5 Fundraising Accelerator Deliverables

Participants progressing into the Accelerator may receive support relating to:

(a) Fundraising Strategy Development;

(b) Fundraising Action Plans;

(c) Investor Universe Mapping;

(d) Investor Prioritisation Matrices;

(e) Investor Outreach Plans;

(f) Investor Target Lists;

(g) Fundraising Data Room preparation guidance;

(h) Investor Engagement Support;

(i) Fundraising CRM development guidance;

(j) mentorship and expert reviews; and

(k) such other outputs as InvestoVilla considers appropriate.

=== Page 64 ===
InvestoVilla does not guarantee that any deliverable will result in investment or commercial success.



4.6 Program Duration

Program duration may vary depending on cohort design and participant pathway.

Subject to operational requirements:

(a) the Venture Readiness Clinic may run between two (2) and four (4) weeks;

(b) the Fundraising Accelerator may run between six (6) and twelve (12) weeks;

(c) the entire Program experience may extend to approximately eighteen (18) weeks.

InvestoVilla reserves the right to modify Program timelines, schedules, formats, and duration.



4.7 Delivery Format

The Program may be delivered through virtual, hybrid, asynchronous, synchronous, or in-person formats.

InvestoVilla may modify delivery methods at its discretion where reasonably necessary to improve effectiveness, respond to operational realities,
address participant needs, or manage unforeseen circumstances.



4.8 Progression Between Program Phases

=== Page 65 ===
Admission into the Venture Readiness Clinic shall not automatically entitle a Founder to progression into the Fundraising Accelerator.

Progression decisions shall be determined by InvestoVilla based on factors including:

(a) participation standards;

(b) completion of required assignments;

(c) coachability and responsiveness to feedback;

(d) venture readiness indicators;

(e) demonstrated commitment; and

(f) such additional considerations as InvestoVilla deems relevant.

InvestoVilla's progression decisions shall be final.
